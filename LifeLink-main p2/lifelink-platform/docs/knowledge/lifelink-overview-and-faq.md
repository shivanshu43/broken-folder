# LifeLink Overview and Frequently Asked Questions

## What is LifeLink?

LifeLink is a coordination platform connecting participating hospitals, blood banks, organ centres, and users. It supports resource discovery, structured blood and organ coordination, notifications, and workflow tracking. It is a coordination and decision-support system, not a replacement for healthcare professionals or regulated healthcare processes.

## What can the Smart FAQ Assistant answer?

The assistant can explain approved LifeLink workflows, terminology, role responsibilities, platform rules, and documented operating steps. Its answers should be grounded in approved LifeLink documentation. If the documentation does not support an answer, the assistant should say so instead of guessing.

## Does the assistant show live inventory or request status?

Document retrieval is not a source of live inventory, offer, donor, recipient, or request status. These details must come from LifeLink's authorized application services. When a question requires a live record or an action, use the corresponding LifeLink page or workflow. The assistant must not present documentation or demonstration data as current operational information.

## Does LifeLink guarantee a match?

No. Search results and matching candidates indicate potential coordination opportunities only. Availability can change and must be confirmed by the responsible institution. A displayed candidate is not a guarantee that a request can be fulfilled.

## Does LifeLink make medical or organ-allocation decisions?

No. LifeLink supports coordination and records relevant workflow information. It does not independently diagnose a condition, establish donor eligibility, approve a transfusion, make final clinical compatibility decisions, or determine final organ allocation. Qualified professionals and authorized institutions remain responsible for those decisions.

## What if the answer is not in the documentation?

The assistant should clearly state that it could not find enough approved information, ask a focused clarifying question if that would help, or direct the user to the relevant authorized LifeLink role or workflow. It must not invent policies, screens, facilities, addresses, contacts, inventory levels, or workflow outcomes.
